# Full example: preserve a travel writer's experience

The source is enthusiastic. Keep that opinion and its stated details. A first-person source does not authorize invented visits, dates, hotels, waits, companions, or changed recommendations.

**Before (AI-sounding):**

> I recently spent five unforgettable days in Lisbon, and let me tell you — this city completely stole my heart. From the moment I arrived, I knew I was somewhere truly special.
>
> Nestled along the banks of the Tagus River, Lisbon stands as a vibrant testament to Portugal's enduring spirit, where rich history and modern energy intertwine at every turn. Yes, the famous hills are challenging — my legs certainly felt it! — but every climb rewards you with breathtaking, panoramic views that make it all worthwhile.
>
> No trip would be complete without riding the iconic Tram 28, winding through the city's most historic neighborhoods. And the food? Simply divine. The original pastéis de nata at Pastéis de Belém are a beloved national treasure, and savoring one still warm was a moment I will never forget.
>
> But what truly makes Lisbon special isn't just the sights — it's the feeling. Wander a few steps off the main squares and you'll discover a quieter, more authentic side: sun-drenched alleys, charming tiled facades, and friendly locals going about their daily lives, inviting you to slow down and savor every moment.
>
> Of course, the must-see São Jorge Castle offers stunning views over the rooftops below. The queues can be long, but the experience is absolutely worth it for any history buff or curious traveler.
>
> Would I go back? Absolutely. Lisbon isn't just a place to visit — it's a place to fall in love with, again and again. If you're dreaming of your next getaway, this is one destination that promises memories to last a lifetime. ✨

**Final rewrite:**

> I recently spent five days in Lisbon and loved it from the moment I arrived.
>
> Lisbon sits along the Tagus River, with history and modern city life close together. The hills made my legs ache, but the views made the climbs worthwhile.
>
> Tram 28 winds through historic neighborhoods and belongs on a visit. The food was wonderful. Eating a still-warm pastel de nata at Pastéis de Belém was a moment I won't forget.
>
> Away from the main squares, the city feels quieter: sunny alleys, tiled facades, and friendly locals going about their day. It invites you to slow down.
>
> São Jorge Castle has views over the rooftops. The queues can be long, but I think it's worth visiting if you're interested in history or curious about the city.
>
> I'd go back. I loved Lisbon, and I'd recommend it to someone planning their next trip.

**Editing check:** The rewrite keeps five days, the Tagus, hills and views, Tram 28 as a recommendation rather than a claimed ride, the warm pastry at Pastéis de Belém, quieter streets, the castle's general queue warning, and the author's enthusiasm. It removes promotional phrasing, chatbot hooks, dashes, and the emoji. It adds no date, accommodation, measured wait, or personal castle visit.
