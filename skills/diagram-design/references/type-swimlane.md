# Swimlane

Ownership and handoffs through a workflow.

Lanes represent accountable actors, teams, or systems, and nodes stay in the lane that performs the action. The flow crosses lanes at actual handoffs. Choose one progression direction and place lane labels on its perpendicular axis. Decisions and loops retain flowchart semantics; lane crossings alone do not imply a message or completion. Reserve gutters for cross-lane connectors and distinguish parallel work from sequential handoff.
