# Quadrant / 2×2 matrix

Two dimensions or four categorical scenarios.

Name both axes and their directions. In a quantitative quadrant, points retain measured coordinates and dividing thresholds are stated (median, target, zero, or another rule). Quadrant membership must follow those thresholds, not editorial preference.

A categorical 2×2 scenario matrix instead crosses two binary conditions. Each cell describes that combination; its equal area is structural, not a probability or magnitude. The consultant variant uses compact titles and supporting implications in each cell, with axis endpoints clearly labeled. Do not imply precise numeric placement in a qualitative matrix.
