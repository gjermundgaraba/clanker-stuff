# Pyramid / funnel

Hierarchy tiers or stage drop-off.

A pyramid's apex means rare, specialized, or high-priority tiers; its base is foundational. A funnel follows a population through successive stages. State the orientation and meaning rather than relying on shape alone.

Conceptual tiers may use schematic trapezoids labeled as non-quantitative. For measured funnels, choose and state the encoding: widths proportional to counts are common (`w_i=W*v_i/max(v)`), but do not describe their trapezoid areas as proportional too. Retention/conversion rates use explicit denominators; stages need the same cohort or a stated population change. Do not force counts to decrease when the actual process admits new entrants.
