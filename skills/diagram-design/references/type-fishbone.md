# Fishbone / cause-and-effect

Organize candidate causes of a named effect.

Put the effect at the head of a spine and attach category ribs; specific candidate causes branch from their category. Categories may reflect the domain rather than a fixed list. Separate observations, hypotheses, and verified causes in wording or notation. The structure organizes causal investigation but does not prove causation or assign quantitative weights. Keep labels outside intersecting ribs and give each cause an unambiguous parent.
