# Org chart / responsibility map

Reporting and accountability.

Place reporting levels vertically and peers on the same rank. Solid reporting lines and dotted advisory or matrix relationships need explicit meanings. Distinguish people from roles, vacant positions, and teams. A role with several reports is not necessarily the owner of every activity those reports perform. Size boxes for actual names and titles; symmetric branches are a layout choice, not evidence of equal headcount or authority.
