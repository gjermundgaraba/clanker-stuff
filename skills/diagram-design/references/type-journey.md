# User journey

A persona’s experience through stages.

Columns are experience stages; rows can show actions, touchpoints, thoughts, pain points, and opportunities. Ground feelings in supplied research or label them as hypotheses. A sentiment trace can connect named ordinal levels; it is not a continuous measured score unless actual measurement supports that. Align each observation with its stage and persona. Multiple personas need visibly separate tracks when their experiences differ. A process without experience evidence need not acquire an invented emotional curve.
